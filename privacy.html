Privacy policy

Your data, clearly explained. What stays on your device and what happens when you contact us.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

An archive on your phone

Pastilo does not require an account. Meals, lists, recorded purchases, prices, notes, errands, optional names and preferences stay on the device. They are not sent to Pastilo servers or RevenueCat. Plus purchase data is handled separately. The app does not integrate advertising or behavioural analytics.

Permissions and reminders

Android notifications are optional and permission can be withdrawn in system settings. Meal, shopping and errand reminders are generated locally and may show text you entered. Lock-screen visibility depends on Android settings.

Retention and deletion

The archive stays on your device until deleted. Local revisions are not an external backup and Android automatic backup is disabled. Uninstalling, clearing app data or a device failure can erase the archive. Errands are managed separately from the meal plan. If you share a .pastilo attachment, the recipient and the sending app may retain a copy.

Support and purposes

If you email support we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send to answer your request. Do not send clinical plans, health information, passwords or unnecessary data about other people. Service-related support is based on fulfilling your request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)); legal obligations and rights requests on Article 6(1)(c). Retention for specific disputes is limited to protecting rights (Article 6(1)(f)). Emails are retained to handle the request and applicable obligations; unnecessary messages are deleted.

Website and external providers

The site is prepared for GitHub Pages. The hosting provider processes technical data such as IP address, request time and browser information to deliver and protect the service. Email contact uses Gmail. Providers may process data outside the EEA under their policies and applicable safeguards. The app does not transmit your local archive to these services.

Rights and contact

The data controller is an individual, the independent developer behind Pastilo, who works under the name RSC Experience. For data processed by the controller, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection where applicable, and complain to your supervisory authority. There is no automated decision-making or profiling. Write to the contact below: we normally respond within one month, subject to justified extensions allowed by GDPR. We cannot retrieve an archive stored only on your device.

Subscriptions and purchase data

Where available, Google Play manages Plus purchases, payments and subscriptions. RevenueCat uses a pseudonymous installation identifier and information about products, receipts and subscription status to verify, restore and manage access. Pastilo does not receive card details. Processing needed to provide and manage Plus is based on performing the requested service (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Administrative records may be kept as required by law. See the Google Privacy Policy and RevenueCat Privacy Policy for provider retention and handling.

Pastilo Coach and client records

Coach saves people, plans, optional measurements, notes and visits in the folder selected by the professional. The portal does not upload those records to Pastilo servers. The browser stores directory access and a local signing key. Selected .pastilo exports may contain personal or health information even when they omit the person’s name. Before sending, verify the recipient and channel. The professional or their organisation identifies the controller and applicable lawful bases, including any required GDPR Article 9 condition, provides notices to clients and manages access, security, retention and rights. Requests about client plans should be directed to the organisation that manages the Coach archive.
