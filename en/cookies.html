This information concerns the website

This page concerns the Pastilo website. The app archive and permissions are described separately in the privacy policy.

No cookies or tracking

The website code sets no cookies and uses no tracking pixels, fingerprinting or analytics. Fonts, images and scripts are local. Dismissing the notice saves pastilo.cookieNotice.dismissed.v1 = 1 in sessionStorage for the tab session. This value does not identify visitors and is not sent to a server.

Your choices

The “Cookie info” tab reopens the notice: it is not a request for tracking consent. The value lasts for the tab session, including browser session restores. If sessionStorage is blocked, dismissal works on the current page. Pastilo uses localStorage to remember the selected language on this device across the website and Coach. Coach can also remember the last working folder through IndexedDB. You can clear these preferences in your browser settings. Without JavaScript, content and links remain readable.

Coach also keeps a signing key in IndexedDB. Clearing site data removes browser preferences, remembered access and the key. Files in the chosen workspace folder remain in that folder.

Hosting, cache and links

GitHub Pages receives technical connection data to deliver and protect the service. No cookies does not mean no personal data processing. Your browser may cache resources. External links open separate services with their own policies; these are not embedded in this site.
