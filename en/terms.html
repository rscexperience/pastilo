The terms for organising meals and shopping with Pastilo.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

What Pastilo does

Pastilo organises user-selected meals, shopping lists and recorded purchases. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnoses, diets or personalised nutritional advice. When transcribing a professional plan, check foods and quantities against the original.

Your choices and content

You are responsible for data you enter and permissions for other people’s content. Meal quantities are totals to prepare, without implicit multiplication by the number of people. Prices are optional: the overview is not complete accounting and does not estimate missing costs.

Availability and storage

Pastilo works on Android without a Pastilo account or plan synchronisation. Errands and manual shopping are available in Free. Where offered, monthly and annual Plus subscriptions unlock meal planning and related features. Google Play shows the price and billing period before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled in Google Play subscription settings. The optional local 14-day trial requires no payment and does not renew. Buying Plus during the trial is a separate choice charged immediately under the store terms. Uninstallation, data clearing or device failure can erase the local archive.

Use and limitations

Use the app in accordance with the law and others’ rights. Reminders depend on system permissions and restrictions and do not guarantee delivery at an exact time. Mandatory statutory rights remain unaffected; these terms do not exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.
